# CircuIT Lab Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

CircuIT Lab is an educational circuit-building game developed by senqx. This policy covers the official Android app, package `senqx.circuitlab`, version 1.0.0.

## Information we collect

The Android app does not collect or transmit personal information, gameplay activity, circuit designs, or device identifiers to the developer. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or a developer-operated crash-reporting service. Circuit simulation and lesson checking run on your device. The game can be played offline.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves your circuits, lesson progress, earned stars, and game preferences locally so you can resume playing. Circuit files may contain labels or other text you choose to enter. This information is used to provide the game and is not sent to the developer.

Local saves remain until you clear the app’s storage, remove the app, or replace them through gameplay. Android backup and device-transfer services may back up or restore app data depending on your device settings. Those services are operated by your operating-system provider, not by senqx.

## Opening, saving, and sharing circuit files

Opening and saving files are optional actions you initiate through Android’s system document picker. The app accesses the file you select or the destination you choose; it does not request broad access to your device’s storage.

When you choose Share, the app creates a circuit file in its cache and passes it to the application you select through Android’s share sheet. That recipient may process or upload the file under its own privacy policy. A storage provider selected in the document picker may also store files in the cloud. The developer does not receive these files automatically.

Temporary sharing files may remain in the app cache until Android removes them or you clear the cache. Exported files and copies held by other apps or storage providers remain until you remove them separately.

## Third-party services

Google Play and Android may process information independently when you download, update, back up, or use the app, according to Google’s policies and your settings. If you send a support request, the developer receives the information you choose to provide and uses it to answer or investigate the request. Do not include passwords, tokens, or private information in public support reports.

## Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from players, including children. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or in-app social features. Optional file sharing uses other applications selected by the player; parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of those services where appropriate.

## Your choices

You can play offline, choose whether to open or share files, and clear local saves through Android’s app-storage settings. You can clear the cache to remove temporary sharing files. Removing local data does not remove files you exported, copies you shared, or backups managed by other services. There is no developer-held gameplay account or player-data database to delete.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change and revise the effective date. Features such as advertising, analytics, accounts, or online storage would require a new review of these practices and disclosures.
